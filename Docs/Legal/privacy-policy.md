# Cadence Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 11 October 2026

Cadence is a wellness app for iPhone that helps you log meals, water and mood, and shows how they add up to a daily stress score. It is developed by Hari (Eidovis). This policy explains what the app does with your data. The short version: your data lives on your phone, there are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and only two narrow things ever leave your device.

## What stays on your device

Everything you enter or that the app computes is stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage:

- Meals, serving sizes, water, coffee and tea entries, streaks
- Mood check-ins and morning and evening check-in answers
- Goals, body metrics (weight, height), fasting schedules and fasting history
- Stress scores, factor breakdowns and insights

If you add the Cadence stress widget to your Home Screen, the app shares the current score with the widget through Apple's app-group storage on the same device. Nothing is synced to a server. Cadence has no user accounts.

## Apple Health

With your permission, Cadence reads from Apple Health: steps, active energy, exercise minutes, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, blood pressure, respiratory rate, time in daylight, dietary water, sleep and State of Mind. With your permission, Cadence writes one thing to Apple Health: your mood check-ins, saved as State of Mind entries.

Health data is read on your device, used only to compute the screens you see, and stored only on your device. It is never sent to the developer or to any third party, and it is never used for advertising or marketing. You can change or revoke Health access at any time in the Health app under Sharing, Apps and Services.

## The two things that leave your device

1. **Meal descriptions, for nutrition estimates.** When you log a meal by typing or by voice, the app sends the text of the meal description and the serving size to Groq, Inc. (api.groq.com), which returns calorie and macronutrient estimates. Only that text is sent: no name, no email, no Health data, no body metrics, and no identifier that links the request to you. Groq's handling of API requests is described in Groq's privacy policy at https://groq.com/privacy-policy.
2. **Barcodes, for product lookup.** When you scan a packaged food, the app sends the barcode number to Open Food Facts (world.openfoodfacts.org), a non-profit open database, to fetch the product's nutrition facts. Nothing else is sent.

Both requests happen only when you log a meal, and both use encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

## Voice logging

Voice entries are converted to text using Apple's on-device speech recognition. Audio is processed on your iPhone and is not sent to the developer or to any third party. The resulting text is then handled as a meal description, as described above.

## On-device intelligence

On devices with Apple Intelligence (iOS 26 and later), daily insights and report narratives are generated on your device. No data is sent anywhere for this feature. On other devices the app uses built-in rules instead.

## Camera and microphone

The camera is used only to scan food barcodes; no photos are taken or stored. The microphone is used only while you are recording a voice meal entry. Both permissions can be withdrawn at any time in Settings, and the rest of the app keeps working.

## Notifications

Fasting reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Cadence does not use push notifications and has no server to send them from.

## Analytics, advertising and tracking

Cadence contains no analytics, no crash reporting service, no advertising SDK and no tracking. It does not use the Apple advertising identifier and does not track you across other apps or websites.

## Deleting your data

Deleting Cadence from your iPhone deletes all data the app stored. Mood entries that Cadence wrote to Apple Health remain in the Health app, where you can view and delete them yourself. Because there are no accounts and no servers, there is nothing to delete on the developer's side.

## Children

Cadence is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
